03 / The meeting point

An instrument.
Many possibilities.

At the heart of the Bösendorfer Performance Lab is the Bösendorfer Disklavier Edition: the meeting of a Viennese piano tradition and Yamaha’s Disklavier Enspire technology.

MUK students and teachers explore its creative possibilities through artistic and scientific work. This portal brings together browser-based tools, experiments and encounters around that practice.
